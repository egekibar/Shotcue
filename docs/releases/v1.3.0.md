Shotcue 1.3.0 speaks English and Turkish, filters the library by status, and lets you pause, resume and delete the transcription model download.

## New

- **English and Turkish.** Shotcue follows your system language, and opens in English for any other language. Pick one yourself in **Settings → General → Language** (*System language*, *English* or *Türkçe*) and choose **Restart now**; macOS's per-app language setting works too. Dates and times follow your region: day-month order and the 12- or 24-hour clock.
- **Voice notes in any language** Whisper knows, or **Detect automatically** (Settings → Audio → Language).
- **Summaries in your language.** Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity write their summary in the interface's language.
- **Filter by status.** The filter button next to the sort button narrows a project or the inbox to one or more statuses, in the grid and the list. The subtitle then reads "3 / 12 items".
- **Pause, resume and delete the transcription model.** Settings → Audio offers **Pause** while it downloads and **Resume** afterwards, also after a restart, picking up from the partial files. **Delete Model** or **Delete Download** frees the disk, after a confirmation.
- **Close a run's log.** A click on the open run in the inspector hides its log; a chevron shows which one is open.

## Changed

- **Clearer hand-off buttons:** *Continue in Terminal*, *Continue in Claude* (resumes the run's session with its history), *Start Over in Claude* (a new session with the prompt filled in) and *Show Changes*. Each has a tooltip, and *Continue in Claude* is hidden for Codex and Antigravity runs.
- **Schedule…** has quick picks (*In 1 hour*, *This evening*, *Tomorrow morning*), a calendar, a typed time and a summary line. A time in the past cannot be scheduled.

## Fixed

- **Continue in Claude** (formerly *Desktop'ta aç*) opens the session in Claude Desktop again; recent Claude Desktop versions ignored the old link.
- The schedule picker no longer breaks its label into one letter per line.
- The library's sort button shows the current order again.
- Clicking the library no longer frames the whole pane in green; a click on empty space or Esc clears the selection.
- A selected card's ring no longer covers its thumbnail.

## Upgrading from 1.2.x

The app offers this version on its own: choose **Update** in the update window, or check by hand from the menu bar.

- **The interface now follows your system language.** If your Mac is set to English, Shotcue opens in English after the update; to keep Turkish, choose **Türkçe** in Settings → General → Language and **Restart now**.
- **Voice notes stay Turkish** unless you had picked another language.

Builds are ad-hoc signed, so macOS asks for Screen Recording and Microphone again after updating. If a switch in System Settings is already on but Shotcue still asks, remove Shotcue from that list (–) and grant it again.

## Install

- **Homebrew:** `brew install --cask egekibar/tap/shotcue`
- **DMG:** download `Shotcue-1.3.0.dmg` below, open it and drag **Shotcue** onto **Applications**. The build is not notarized: allow the first launch in System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Open Anyway**, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Shotcue.app`.

Requirements: macOS 26 or later, Apple Silicon, and at least one coding agent CLI logged in: Claude Code (`claude`, with a Claude subscription), Codex (`codex`, logged in with ChatGPT) or Antigravity (`agy`).

## Türkçe özet

Shotcue 1.3.0 artık **Türkçe ve İngilizce**: sistem dilini takip eder (başka dillerde İngilizce açılır), Ayarlar → Genel → **Dil**'den seçilip **Şimdi yeniden başlat** ile değiştirilebilir. Sesli notlar Whisper'ın bildiği her dilde ya da otomatik algılamayla yazıya dökülür; ajanlar özetlerini arayüz dilinde yazar. Kütüphaneye **durum filtresi** geldi; transkripsiyon modelinin indirmesi **duraklatılıp sürdürülebiliyor** ve model silinebiliyor; inspector'da bir çalışmanın logu tekrar tıklanınca kapanıyor. Aktarım düğmelerinin adları netleşti (*Claude'da devam et*, *Claude'da baştan başlat*, *Değişiklikleri göster*), *Zamanla…* penceresi yenilendi; *Claude'da devam et* yeniden çalışıyor ve kütüphanedeki görsel hatalar düzeltildi.

**Güncellerken:** Mac'in dili İngilizceyse Shotcue güncellemeden sonra İngilizce açılır; Türkçe kalsın istersen Ayarlar → Genel → Dil'den **Türkçe**'yi seçip **Şimdi yeniden başlat**'a bas. Sesli notlar, başka bir dil seçmediysen Türkçe kalır. Build ad-hoc imzalı olduğundan güncellemeden sonra Ekran Kaydı ve Mikrofon izinleri yeniden istenir.
